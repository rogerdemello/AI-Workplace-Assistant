# MARK Product Overview

## What is MARK?

MARK is an AI-powered HR operating system that replaces traditional HR workflows with a conversational assistant.

Instead of filling forms, raising tickets manually, or waiting for HR responses, employees talk to Mark as they would talk to a real HR partner.

## Employee Experience

MARK is a friendly HR companion available anytime.

Employees can use MARK to:

- Raise complaints (confidentially when needed)
- Apply for leave step-by-step in chat
- Ask HR policy questions and get instant guidance
- Set reminders (medication, meetings, personal follow-ups)
- Get emotional support when stressed
- Share feedback naturally without formal surveys

No forms. No portals. Just conversation.

## System Perspective

MARK is a decision engine that converts conversations into actions.

Every chat is automatically:

- Understood (intent detection)
- Structured (workflow mapping)
- Stored (tickets, logs, wellbeing signals)
- Analyzed (sentiment, engagement, risk)

## HR Perspective

MARK is a real-time intelligence dashboard for workforce health.

HR teams see:

- Sentiment trends
- Attrition risk signals
- Manager-related issue clusters
- Engagement scores
- Silent/disengaged employee patterns
- Repeated complaint trends

MARK shifts HR from reactive operations to proactive support.

## What Makes MARK Different

### 1) Conversational First

Everything starts with natural chat.

### 2) Human-Like HR Partner

MARK is designed to feel supportive and contextual:

- empathetic tone
- concise responses
- context retention
- emotional support behaviors

### 3) Intelligent Automation

Chat to action, automatically.

Example:

`"I need leave next week"` -> leave request created -> manager notified -> HR updated.

### 4) Continuous Listening

MARK continuously detects:

- frustration
- burnout
- disengagement
- potential attrition risk

### 5) Proactive HR System

MARK does not wait for problems. It can:

- send check-ins
- trigger reminders and nudges
- alert HR early
- suggest interventions

## Simple Flow

Employee chats
-> MARK detects intent
-> Workflow is executed (ticket, leave, policy guidance, reminders)
-> Sentiment/activity signals are stored
-> HR dashboard updates
-> Alerts and insights are generated

## Positioning

MARK turns employee conversations into HR actions and insights.

Simple shorthand:

Slack + HR + AI + therapist-style support + ticketing automation = MARK

B2B positioning:

An AI-powered Chief Listening Officer that continuously understands, supports, and acts on employee needs through conversation.
