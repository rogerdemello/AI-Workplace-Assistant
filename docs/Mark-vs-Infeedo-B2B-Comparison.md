# MARK vs Infeedo
## High-Level B2B Comparison

Version: 1.0  
Date: April 15, 2026  
Status: Positioning Reference

## 1. Core Positioning

### Infeedo
Employee engagement, surveys, and sentiment analytics platform.

Primary focus:
- Listening to employees
- Measuring sentiment and happiness
- Reporting insights to HR

### MARK
AI HR operating system with a conversation-first interface.

Primary focus:
- Talking to employees continuously
- Executing HR work and workflows
- Automating actions and follow-ups
- Analyzing trends and risk

### Core Difference
Infeedo listens.  
MARK listens and acts.

## 3. Product Philosophy

### Infeedo
Survey-first model:

Ask -> Collect -> Analyze -> Report

### MARK
Conversation-first model:

Talk -> Understand -> Act -> Analyze -> Improve

## 4. Why MARK Can Win

### 4.1 Lower Friction Experience
Infeedo depends heavily on survey participation. MARK uses natural chat, reducing response friction and supporting continuous engagement.

### 4.2 Full-Loop Actionability
Infeedo identifies issues. MARK can identify, create a ticket, alert HR, and follow up in one loop.

### 4.3 More Human Interaction Model
Infeedo interaction is structured and survey-centric. MARK is conversational, emotional, and memory-driven.

### 4.4 Stronger Proactive Behavior
MARK can proactively check in, nudge breaks, and follow up unresolved HR issues as part of normal operation.

## 5. B2B Value Proposition

### For HR Leaders
- Infeedo delivers insight.
- MARK delivers insight plus action plus automation.

### For Employees
- Infeedo experience: "I fill surveys."
- MARK experience: "I talk to someone that helps me."

## 7. Positioning Statement

MARK is an AI HR operating system that goes beyond engagement tools like Infeedo by not only understanding employee sentiment but also taking real-time actions, automating HR workflows, and providing a continuous, human-like conversational experience for employees.

## 8. Competitive Edge Summary

MARK combines capabilities usually spread across multiple tools:
- Engagement intelligence layer
- Ticketing execution
- HRMS-lite actions
- Conversational AI assistant

Unified in one chat-first interface.

## 9. Final Comparison

Infeedo: intelligence layer.  
MARK: intelligence plus execution layer.
